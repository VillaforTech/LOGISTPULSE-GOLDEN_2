# Student missions

## Inventory team
Design and implement replenishment rules and an inventory business metric.

## Distribution team
Add route/deviation rules and cold-chain alerting.

## Smart Operations team
Add equipment thresholds, incident lifecycle and MQTT reliability behavior.

## Fulfillment team
Add kitchen capacity, SLA risk and event-driven order transitions.

Every team must deliver: C4 delta, ADR, code, tests, CI evidence, Docker health, Prometheus metric and Grafana evidence.
